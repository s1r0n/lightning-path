# Myths and Symbols

<img src="https://spiritwiki.lightningpath.org/images/thumb/c/ca/World.jpg/642px-World.jpg?20171022131007" width=250 align=right>Myths and Symbols is a curated collection of cultural works—parables, allegories, poems, videos, monographs, and more—that express and explore the core truths of the [Avatar.GLOBAL](https://spiritwiki.lightningpath.org/index.php/Avatar.GLOBAL) cosmology and the [Lightning Path Human Development Framework](https://spiritwiki.lightningpath.org/index.php/Lightning_Path_Human_Development_Framework). These cultural artifacts serve not just as reflections of the system’s principles, but as living symbols that will hopefully inspire healing, emancipation, and reconnection, If you have work rooted in these ideas, or if you are inspired to create something new, we invite you to contribute. Contact Dr. Mike Sosteric at mikes@athabascau.ca to share your vision and have it included in this repository.

This directory should rightly be put at the end of this repository after the empirical and theoretical foundations are in place, but many artists may want to just jump into the symbols and narratives first. Feel free. You can always read through the materials when you're ready.  

If you have a resource you'd like to see shared here, contact mikes@athabascau.ca
