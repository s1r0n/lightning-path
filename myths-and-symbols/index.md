## Myths and Symbols

<img src="https://spiritwiki.lightningpath.org/images/thumb/c/ca/World.jpg/642px-World.jpg?20171022131007" width=250 align=right>Myths and Symbols is a curated collection of cultural works—parables, allegories, poems, videos, monographs, and more—that express and explore the core truths of the Avatar.GLOBAL cosmology and the Lightning Path human development framework. These pieces serve not just as reflections of the system’s principles, but as living symbols that will hopefully inspire healing, emancipation, and reconnection, If you have work rooted in these ideas, or if you're inspired to create something new, we invite you to contribute. Contact Dr. Mike Sosteric at mikes@athabascau.ca to share your vision.
