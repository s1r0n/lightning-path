<h1>This Morning</h1>
This morning opened up my eyes,<br>
Realized my life is full of lies.<br>
While in my bed they swam through head<br>
And dragged me far from bright Godhead.

My mom, my dad, my teachers lied;<br>
The priest, the Hindu guru guy.<br>
And Hollywood? Well they’re the worst.<br>
They even beat out Catholic Church.

And ask myself, as sadness rise.<br>
How did the world become so vile?<br>
How could my parents smile and think<br>
The things they did just didn’t stink.

But then I realize, just today,<br>
I told a lie and walked away.<br>
Did not think twice of this small vice.<br>
I’m sure it made the world more nice.

And this I do most every day,<br>
And though it turns my heart to clay.<br>
I just won’t tell the truth I know.<br>
For doing so would spoil the show.

Mike Sosteric
