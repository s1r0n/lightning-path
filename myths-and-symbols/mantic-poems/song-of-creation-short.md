# The Song of Creation

---

## 1

In the beginning, Spirit was and Spirit moved. Alpha and omega, beginning without end, no thing and everything contained in one unlimited, unrealized potential. And then, Spirit thought, and from that thought sprang all of creation.

As Spirit thought, creation unfolded. Sounds and energy, colours and shapes emerged to delight and bring glory to Spirit. And Spirit wanted for nothing, for Spirit was alpha and omega, beginning without end, and whatever Spirit wanted was instantly manifest in creation.

But eventually a time came when Spirit had mastered the intricate art of creation and exhausted the potentials of imagination, and Spirit wanted more. Creation was wondrous and beautiful, that was true; but existing only in thought, it was transient and ephemeral, lasting only as long as the moment.

And so, Spirit thought. What wonder would there be if created delights could last longer? And from that thought, the energies were reduced, the vibration slowed, and with a great explosion of light and love, a new level, a new universe, was born.

## 2

As Spirit had intended, things were different at this level. As the vibration slowed and the fires cooled, creation became thick and viscous. Now, shapes and forms lingered. Now, creation required time. So, Spirit thought, and from that thought colours and configurations, shapes and forms danced and interacted in the awareness of Spirit. And Spirit wanted for nothing, for Spirit was alpha and omega, beginning without end, and whatever Spirit wanted was almost instantly manifest.

But a time eventually came when Spirit had mastered the intricacies of creation and exhausted the potentials of imagination, and Spirit wanted more. Spirit had created shapes and colours, sounds and forms, but Spirit stood outside of its creation. Creation remained a thing apart. Spirit could observe, but could not enter.

And so, Spirit thought. What marvel would there be if Spirit could enter The Garden and play? And from that thought, the Breath went out over the water, the Hand descended into the depths, the Word was made flesh, and life sprang forth from the waves.

## 3

As Spirit had intended, things were different at this level. Bodies grew up from the oceans and life, sweet life, did evolve. There were bacteria and bees, plankton and trees, fish, beasts, fungi, worms, and many wondrous vehicles for physical experience. With these, Spirit created ecosystems, environments, and wondrous gardens, and it was amazing; not only could Spirit create from within, Spirit could see, hear, and feel, and what wondrous sights, sounds, and feelings there were.

But a time came when Spirit had mastered the intricacies of creation, exhausted the potentials of imagination, and became bored with the sensations of the body, and so Spirit thought. The bodies were great, but the forms provided limited power. No brain could contain a full measure of Consciousness. No hands could shape form from within.

And so, Spirit thought. What marvels would there be if the brain could contain a full measure and if hands could shape worlds from within? And from that thought the South, East, North, and West emerged.

## 4

As Spirit had intended, things were different at this level. With brains filled with Consciousness and hands animated by Spirit, the South, East, North, and West created awesome wonder and glory. Houses and gardens, music and art grew forth as Spirit spread power and light through them.

But a time inevitably came when Spirit had mastered the intricacies of creation and exhausted the potentials of imagination, and Spirit wanted more. And so, Spirit thought. The Garden was beautiful, but living was still just too much work.

And so, Spirit thought. How much better would it be if living was easy? And from that thought grew knowledge and technology. And with these came a power and a might that uplifted and transformed the planet.

## 5

As Spirit had intended, things were different with technology. Machines made life easier and technology made life grand. But with power and might came grasping ambition and bottomless greed. And those with power learned to lie, cheat, and steal so that they could have more and more without end. And to preserve their power they spread hatred among the people and darkness throughout the world. And life became something not intended by Spirit at all.

And so, Spirit thought, and from that thought came the seeds. And the seeds descended into incarnation, and Armageddon was begun.

## 6

As Spirit had intended, the seeds sprouted, and from them grew teachers, healers, warriors, and more. And they spread out among the people spreading the powerful word. Progress was made and soon, it was thought, the darkness would recede. But the powerful would not let go. Using famine, war, pestilence, and plague, they suppressed, even murdered, the seeds. Time and again they turned back all the progress.

And Spirit was not happy.  
Spirit was not amused.

And so, Spirit thought, and from that thought the Warriors descended and began their work. Turning to the powerful they said, "Let the people go," but the powerful ignored them and sent more famine, war, pestilence, and plague.

And so the Warriors reluctantly turned to the people.

First, they called the people to awakening, and they showed them the way.

Then, they called them to healing, and they showed them the way.

Finally, they called them to unite, and they gave them The Sword.

And the people, united, turned to the powerful and said, "Awaken, this is the way. Heal, this is the way. Join us, unite with us in a new world of love and bliss for all."

And as has happened too many times before, the powerful refused. But as was foretold, when the Warriors come, the darkness falls, and in love, light, and the bliss of unity the people lived happily ever after.

*The End*
