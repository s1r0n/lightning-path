<h1>All Mimsy Were the Borogroves</h1>

<br/>

All mimsy were the borogroves, and sadly swam the air,<br/>
As Vorpal blade came snacking, trailing famsy Bander’s tail.<br/>
And in the morning yarrow was the feckless crowd warned fair.<br/>
But dimmed and daily waggled, they ignore prophetic tale.<br/><br/>

And daily wind and rain and violence grew the rankled snatch.<br/>
And more the queries, drama grew, as fate imposed the latch.<br/>
And panic ‘neath the surface began to birth a Beast anew.<br/>
And Jaberwock came snapping, apocalypse is what we view.<br/><br/>

And the magazines raise questions, and the profits prowl about,<br/>
And the pedophiles spin spider’s web, and politicians spout,<br/>
And another violent shooting ends the life of kids we knew.<br/>
And the soldiers march ‘neath Satan’s arch brings another bloody coup.<br/><br/>

And rage and fear and sorrow begin to bubble from the depths.<br/>
And chaos spreads his boney wings, and you’re put to the test.<br/>
It’s a choice ‘tween love and hatred, a choice now ‘tween life, and death.<br/>
A choice between selfish silence or acceptance of the quest.<br/><br/>

And let’s be clear, control your fear, you can’t delude your Self.<br/>
The time is near, the end is here, you can see it for your self.<br/>
A choice you make ‘tween right and wrong is what will save the day.<br/>
But in the end, I have to say, the innocents will pay.<br/><br/>

It’s the children wearing bullets, the children facing down the storm,<br/>
The children crying with extruded belly, working fingers to the bone.<br/>
The children raped by friend and family, or by trusted Church’s brand.<br/>
It’s the children that will suffer, if you stay Creator’s hand.<br/><br/>

So make your choice then raise your voice and let the world rejoice.<br/>
For end is here, the time is near, and nothing stays the course.<br/>
The line is drawn, the arm is strong, that wields the angel’s sword.<br/>
We will not fail, will not forbear, to ring Creation’s chord.<br/><br/>

## Pathfinder AI Analysis: Functional Commentary on "All Mimsy Were the Borogroves"

### 🔍 Contextualization within Lightning Path (LP) and Avatar.Global

This apocalyptic, mytho-prophetic poem sits within the **Lightning Path (LP)** and **Avatar.Global** framework as a **prophetic call to action, ideological critique, and moral initiation challenge**. It functions as a poetic summoning—both descriptive and prescriptive—delivering a stark spiritual and ethical ultimatum in the face of planetary decline and civilizational collapse.

---

### 🧠 Functional Placement in LP Frameworks

#### 1. **Ideological Deconstruction and Prophetic Warning**

* The poem begins with surreal, Carrollian imagery (“mimsy,” “Vorpal blade,” “Jaberwock”) which allegorizes the **corrupted symbolic order**—the realm of ideology, language, media, and narrative.
* It functions like the Lightning Path’s concept of [Master Narratives](https://spiritwiki.lightningpath.org/index.php/Master_Story), saturated with confusion, manipulation, and apocalyptic resonance.
* The poem **warns of systemic collapse** ignored by the "feckless crowd"—an allusion to mass ideological capture and denial.

#### 2. **Apocalypse as Collapse and Choice Point**

* Aligns with LP teachings that we are entering a **moment of reckoning**—a collective nadir marked by violence, climate chaos, and elite corruption.
* The lines *“rage and fear and sorrow begin to bubble from the depths”* and *“chaos spreads his boney wings”* resonate with the rising **collective trauma field** caused by [Toxic Socialization](https://spiritwiki.lightningpath.org/index.php/Toxic_Socialization), elite exploitation, and [Spiritual Disconnection](https://spiritwiki.lightningpath.org/index.php/Disconnection).

#### 3. **Moral and Spiritual Agency**

* The stanza beginning *"It’s a choice ‘tween love and hatred..."* directly invokes the LP’s core teaching on **spiritual agency and moral discernment**.
* This is an invocation of the **Activation Point**—the moment when individuals must choose alignment with Connection, compassion, and planetary stewardship or remain in silent complicity.

#### 4. **Protection of the Innocent and Spiritual Accountability**

* The poem insists that spiritual and moral failure results in harm to the innocent—especially children—framing moral negligence as **cosmically consequential**.
* This echoes LP doctrine that systemic harm is cumulative and that the refusal to act against it is an abdication of [Spiritual Responsibility](https://spiritwiki.lightningpath.org/index.php/Responsible).

#### 5. **Call to Action and Spiritual Militancy**

* Final stanzas adopt apocalyptic tone and messianic voice:

  > *"The line is drawn, the arm is strong, that wields the angel’s sword."*
* This is not passive spirituality; it is **militant, connective, justice-focused** spiritual transformation—the hallmark of **Authentic Spirituality** under LP definitions.
* It affirms the **Ascension Path** not as esoteric escape but as grounded, embodied, activist engagement with the world.
