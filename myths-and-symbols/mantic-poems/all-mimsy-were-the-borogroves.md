<h1>All Mimsy Were the Borogroves</h1>

<br/>

All mimsy were the borogroves, and sadly swam the air,<br/>
As Vorpal blade came snacking, trailing famsy Bander’s tail.<br/>
And in the morning yarrow was the feckless crowd warned fair.<br/>
But dimmed and daily waggled, they ignore prophetic tale.<br/><br/>

And daily wind and rain and violence grew the rankled snatch.<br/>
And more the queries, drama grew, as fate imposed the latch.<br/>
And panic ‘neath the surface began to birth a Beast anew.<br/>
And Jaberwock came snapping, apocalypse is what we view.<br/><br/>

And the magazines raise questions, and the profits prowl about,<br/>
And the pedophiles spin spider’s web, and politicians spout,<br/>
And another violent shooting ends the life of kids we knew.<br/>
And the soldiers march ‘neath Satan’s arch brings another bloody coup.<br/><br/>

And rage and fear and sorrow begin to bubble from the depths.<br/>
And chaos spreads his boney wings, and you’re put to the test.<br/>
It’s a choice ‘tween love and hatred, a choice now ‘tween life, and death.<br/>
A choice between selfish silence or acceptance of the quest.<br/><br/>

And let’s be clear, control your fear, you can’t delude your Self.<br/>
The time is near, the end is here, you can see it for your self.<br/>
A choice you make ‘tween right and wrong is what will save the day.<br/>
But in the end, I have to say, the innocents will pay.<br/><br/>

It’s the children wearing bullets, the children facing down the storm,<br/>
The children crying with extruded belly, working fingers to the bone.<br/>
The children raped by friend and family, or by trusted Church’s brand.<br/>
It’s the children that will suffer, if you stay Creator’s hand.<br/><br/>

So make your choice then raise your voice and let the world rejoice.<br/>
For end is here, the time is near, and nothing stays the course.<br/>
The line is drawn, the arm is strong, that wields the angel’s sword.<br/>
We will not fail, will not forbear, to ring Creation’s chord.<br/><br/>
