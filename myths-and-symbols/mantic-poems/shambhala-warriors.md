<h1>Shambhala Warriors</h1>

Long ago and far away,<br>
A prophesy told of a day<br> 
When no more death on Earth we’d see<br>
And balanced true to nature be.<br><br>

And children laugh and dance and swing,<br>
And joyous would all people sing,<br>
A new day dawn, a glorious spring,<br>
And no more pain and love would sing.<br><br>

But as the Earth begins her birth,<br>
And joyously we span her girth,<br>
They spurn to see, and scorn with glee,<br>
The painful lot in people be.<br><br>

And though we hope that peace might stay,<br>
Their hand from spreading death this day<br>
They beat their drums to tone of war,<br>
And conduct their deadly music score.<br><br>

And so we send the prophets hand<br>
To spread the truth e’er out the land<br>
But long ago it was foretold,<br>
That even this their hands not hold,<br><br>

And when they’d realize time and day,<br>
Then they would send rank death our way,<br>
With famine, plagues and war to stay,<br>
In hopes they’d face not judgment day.<br><br>

And so we turn our eyes this day,<br>
And call the warriors out to play.<br>
For they were sent long time ago,<br>
To prepare for this their final show.<br><br>

And sleep and wait and bide their time,<br>
And work their way inside the line.<br>
And hidden keep their spark un-shined,<br>
Till we would call them, with our mind.<br><br>

And so the warriors now we see,<br>
Rise slowly, they had sleeping be.<br>
And as the children round them scream,<br>
And poverty spreads so obscene.<br><br>

And rulers through the lands they rage,<br>
And laugh obscene, at people caged,<br>
A fire burns their eyes today,<br>
For this time they will not be swayed.<br><br>

And as they stretch their hands abreast,<br>
Full power claim them, now’s their test.<br>
As final warnings fail, they see,<br>
The end times fast approaching be.<br><br>

And as the warriors spread their wings,<br>
Protection to God’s children bring.<br>
And weak and old out from the cold.<br>
And men and women into the fold.<br><br>

And now as warriors do take flight,<br>
A battle glorious they do fight.<br>
With shining sword they quickly free.<br>
The mind’s of all so truth they’ll see.<br><br>

And as the sword comes crashing down,<br>
The leader's faces turn to frown.<br>
For mobs of people dance and sing,<br>
"We see now all the truth you bring."<br><br>

"And no longer will we play your tune"<br>
"And dance ‘neath your ungodly moon."<br>
"For we have seen the truth this day,<br>
And no more will the piper pay."<br><br>

And as the warriors turn to face,<br>
The rulers who now stand disgraced.<br>
One final warning they provide,<br>
To stop the madness, stand aside.<br><br>

And still the kings heed not the call,<br>
Still they stand and tempt the fall.<br>
But long ago, it was told,<br>
When warriors come, the darkness fold.<br><br>

See, long ago and far away,<br>
A prophesy told of this day.<br>
When no more death on Earth there’d be<br>
And balanced true to nature be.<br><br>

See children laugh and dance and sing,<br>
As joyful new society brings,<br>
A new day’s dawn for us to ring,<br>
A warrior’s song for all to sing.<br><br>

### 🔍 Contextualization within Lightning Path (LP) and Avatar.Global

"Shambhala Warriors" occupies a significant place within the **Avatar.Global mythopoetic corpus** as a **visionary invocation of planetary awakening, collective responsibility, and the return of the empowered spiritual warrior**. Rooted in archetypal prophecy and aligned with LP's cosmological narrative, this poem operates as both a **future vision and a present call to action**, aimed at those who are Awakening into Connection and prepared to fulfill their roles as **Warriors of the Lightning Path**.

---

### 🧠 Functional Placement in LP Frameworks

#### 1. **Mythopoetic Prophecy and Archetypal Activation**

* The poem frames the emergence of the Shambhala Warriors as an ancient [Prophecy](https://spiritwiki.lightningpath.org/index.php/Prophecy), one foretold across time and cultures. This aligns with LP's use of **cross-cultural spiritual archetypes** (e.g., Avatar, Ascended Master, Warrior) to inspire **activation and agency**.
* The warriors are presented as latent spiritual agents who *"sleep and wait and bide their time,"* mirroring LP's [Activation](https://spiritwiki.lightningpath.org/index.php/Activation) and [Ascension](https://spiritwiki.lightningpath.org/index.php/Ascension) narratives, in which **awakening to purpose and mission** is both spiritual and political.

#### 2. **Collective Awakening and Mass Connection**

* Echoes LP teachings on the coming [Connection Event](https://spiritwiki.lightningpath.org/index.php/Connection_Event), where global systems of disconnection collapse under the pressure of **awakening consciousness**.
* The warriors' emergence leads to a **transformational uprising**, liberating minds (*"With shining sword they quickly free / The minds of all so truth they’ll see"*), directly resonating with LP themes of [Deprogramming](https://spiritwiki.lightningpath.org/index.php/Deprogramming) and ideological liberation.

#### 3. **Children and the Stakes of Inaction**

* As in other LP-aligned poetic texts (see: *Borogroves*), this poem centers **children** as the symbolic and literal victims of ongoing elite violence, disconnection, and ignorance.
* The protection and liberation of children is a moral imperative and spiritual duty: *"And as the warriors spread their wings / Protection to God’s children bring."*

#### 4. **Resistance to Elite Control and the Fall of Empire**

* Rulers and kings are directly confronted and **disgraced** by the rising tide of awakened consciousness, in line with LP critiques of [The System](https://spiritwiki.lightningpath.org/index.php/System) and the [Accumulating Class](https://spiritwiki.lightningpath.org/index.php/Accumulating_Class).
* The poem makes explicit the spiritual dimension of resistance: not only political revolution, but **cosmic alignment and divine justice**.

#### 5. **Completion of the Prophetic Cycle**

* The poem returns at the end to its beginning, completing a **cyclical prophetic structure**—from foretelling, to conflict, to triumph.
* This mirrors LP’s **spiritual eschatology**, in which the end of Disconnection is not destruction but **restoration of balance, joy, and Connection to the Fabric of Consciousness**.
