# Monographs

<img src="https://spiritwiki.lightningpath.org/images/8/81/Graduation.jpg" align=right width=250px style="padding: 10px;">This section of the Myths and Symbols archive collects long-form works—essays, treatises, guides, and manuals—that elaborate, express, or apply the principles of the [Avatar.GLOBAL](https://spiritwiki.lightningpath.org/index.php/Avatar.GLOBAL) cosmology and the Lightning Path Human Development Framework. These monographs describe and extend, embody, and contribute to its living evolution. Each entry here is both a symbolic artifact and a practical instrument of healing, reconnection, and systemic emancipation.

If you have written or are inspired to create a monograph that aligns with the [Avatar.GLOBAL](https://spiritwiki.lightningpath.org/index.php/Avatar.GLOBAL) vision—works that are decolonized, healing-oriented, spiritually integrated, and grounded in empirical, psychological, or experiential insight—we welcome your contribution. Submissions may take any form (academic, poetic, experiential, or hybrid) so long as they support the overarching goal of human development and planetary restoration.

To submit your work, contact Dr. Mike Sosteric at mikes@athabascau.ca and share your vision.
