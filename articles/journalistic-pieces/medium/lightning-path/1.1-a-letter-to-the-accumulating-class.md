# A Letter to the Accumulating Class – Let's Talk as Adults

This first open letter challenges the Accumulating Class to face the full implications of their role in systemic planetary collapse. From an LP standpoint, the letter provides a diagnosis of the disconnection-driven logic at the core of capitalism—what LP calls the Regime of Accumulation. It addresses elite toxic socialization, unconscious trauma repetition, and spiritual disconnection as the underlying operating system of accumulation. It invites elites not to guilt, but to *connection*—to upgrade from system lock to ethical clarity, and from asset hoarding to planetary stewardship.
