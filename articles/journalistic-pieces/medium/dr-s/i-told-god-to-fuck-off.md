# I Told God to F*ck Off

This raw and revealing autoethnographic account documents the initial [connection experienc](https://spiritwiki.lightningpath.org/index.php/Connection_Experience)e that catalyzed the development of the Lightning Path system. It describes an intense, cannabis-facilitated mystical encounter that began in terror and judgment—perceived as divine condemnation—but evolved into a liberating confrontation with abusive theological conditioning. The article marks a pivotal moment of egoic collapse, cognitive flooding, and the emergence of what would become a sustained mystical download.
