# A Letter to the Accumulating Class II – You Have a Choice

In this follow-up, the warning becomes stark: planetary collapse is not speculative, it is happening now. From the LP perspective, the failure of the oceans, the breakdown of climate systems, and the intensification of global polycrisis mark the critical consequences of elite disconnection. The letter escalates the moral and existential confrontation, situating the elite not as villains, but as spiritually fragmented actors with a diminishing window for course correction. The message is clear: wake up, reconnect, or perish with the world you helped break.
