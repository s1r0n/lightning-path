#Advanced Study

Welcome to my course repository. These are university-level courses written and coordinated by me, Dr. Mike Sosteric, address topics related to and raised in the LP curriculum. They are offered through Athabasca University, Canada’s premier open university. 

Athabasca is fully accredited and offers global access—anyone in the world can enroll in these courses for academic credit or take them as audit learners. Each course explores cutting-edge topics in sociology, narrative theory, ideology, and symbolic power, blending academic rigour with a commitment to transformative education and planetary healing.

If you have questions about the course content, feel free to email me at mikes@athabascau.ca and I will endeavor to answer your questions.
