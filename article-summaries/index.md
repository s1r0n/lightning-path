## Article Summaries

Despite the protestations of many scholars, myself included, scholarly journal articles remain largely locked behind massive paywalls that only large institutions can unlock. For this reason this space will include detailed summaries of key theoretical and empirical support articles, meaning articles the support the Lightning Paths' human development project. 

 If you'd like to submit a summary, email it to mikes@athabascau.ca. The article should be no longer than 800 words. Use APA for the citation. It should look like this
 
 
 Title
   
 Content
 
 Author Byline 
 
 Citation in APA format
 
 When you write (or generate) the summary, make sure to focus it on topics relevant to the LP corpus.
